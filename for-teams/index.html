How It Works

YourTake does the heavy lifting while your comms team stays in control. It all runs on one thing per leader: a deep, living picture of their voice and their market.

1. A Profile for Every Leader

Every post for an executive is drawn from one rich profile:

  • The company brand as the base layer
  • Each leader's voice, built from their real posts
  • Their stories and areas of expertise
  • How their audience talks, in its own words
  • The proof behind the company's claims
  • Each leader's strategy, where they're headed

Profiles refresh themselves as your site, blog, and press coverage change, so no leader's profile goes stale.

2. On-Brand Content in One Place

Everything your team needs to make a post, all running on the leader's profile:

  • Web research, backed by real sources
  • Writing, in the leader's voice
  • Image generation, in your brand style
  • Carousels, in your colors and layout
  • Infographics, in your colors and style
  • Memes

No juggling five tools, no copy-paste. YourTake drafts, your comms team picks the best version, tweaks it in a sentence, and the comms lead approves before anything goes live.

3. It Adapts Each Post for Every Platform

Each version is reshaped to fit the platform, with correctly sized assets. Your team copies or exports, so you always control what gets posted.
